# Personalized Birthday Surprise Website 🎁

A beautiful, emotional, dreamy pink aesthetic scrapbook birthday surprise website crafted for Ishu's 18th Birthday.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Dreamy Pink Cloud Aesthetic**: Beautiful soft pink cloud sky texture background with floating glowing stars, sparkles, and hearts.
- **Strict Friendship Theme**: Pure, heartfelt best-friend / homegirl & homeboy tribute.
- **Song Added**: **"Adhento Gaani Vunnapaatuga"** (Movie: *JERSEY* • Music by Anirudh Ravichander).
  - Floating interactive YouTube player widget at the bottom right with minimize/expand view.
  - Interactive top navigation music pill.
  - Dedicated vinyl turntable player card on Slide 6.
  - Plays automatically from the beginning (`0:00`) upon unlocking Slide 1 or on clicking play.
- **Centrally Aligned Grand Finale Page (Slide 6)**:
  - Beautifully centered birthday letter, header, blessings, and song player.
- **6 Hand-Crafted Slides**:
  1. **Password Gate 🔐**: Protected with secret code `26072006` with cute clues and shake animations.
  2. **Chapter 1 (Lucky Charm Intro 🌸)**: Childhood photo with heartfelt words.
  3. **Chapter 2 (Her Strength 💪)**: Cherished childhood photo together with words of courage.
  4. **Chapter 3 (Lucky Charm Message 🧿)**: Beautiful floral portrait reminding her how special she is.
  5. **Chapter 4 (Friendship Promise 🤝)**: Elegant portrait with a promise of lifelong friendship.
  6. **Chapter 5 (The Grand Finale 👑)**: Playful best friend warning badge followed by the 18th Birthday letter for Ishu and the song player!
- **Interactive Floating Hearts & Confetti**:
  - Tapping or clicking anywhere on the screen bursts cute floating hearts and stars.
  - Celebration confetti showers on correct unlock and grand finale reveal.
- **Smooth Navigation**: Previous / Next buttons, progress dots, keyboard arrow controls (← / →), and mobile touch-swipe gesture support.
